GoSki at a glance

A free ski app for iPhone, iPad and Android: 3D trail maps of 87 resorts, a GPS ski tracker that names every run, snow forecasts and alerts.

3D trail maps

Every trail, lift and glade at all 87 resorts on real 3D terrain, with Live Lift Status backed by Real Time data. Satellite Overlay drapes the real aerial photo over the map at 76 resorts.

All trail maps

GPS ski tracker

One tap to start, then just ski. GoSki tracks vertical, speed, distance and time, and names every run by the trails you skied.

How the Ski Tracker works

Snow reports and forecasts

New snow, base depth and season totals, the 7-day forecast for every resort, snowfall maps and the Snow leaderboard. Weather from NOAA in North America and Europe’s national weather services in the Alps.

Snow forecasts and reports

Mountain Intel

Wind, Powder Zone, Sun, Snow depth and Surface Conditions on the 3D trail map, hour by hour.

About Mountain Intel

Alerts for your mountains

Storm Alerts from the first outlook to the final snowfall update, a Snow Alert when snow shows up in the forecast, and Day Alerts for lift holds, delays and closures.

How alerts work

Free and GoSki PRO

Free

Snow reports, the 7-day forecast, Ski Tracker Basic, 2D trail maps of every resort plus three free 3D maps, Live Lift Status, snowfall maps, the Snow leaderboard and each storm’s first alert.

GoSki PRO

Adds 3D maps at every resort, Satellite Overlay, Mountain Intel, Ski Tracker PLUS, Live Radar, Day Alerts, every Storm Alert and text message alerts. A weekly or monthly subscription; prices are in the app.
