Telluride Ski Resort questions

Where can I check the Telluride Ski Resort snow forecast?

In GoSki, free on the App Store and Google Play. The Telluride Ski Resort forecast gives snow, temperature and wind from the National Weather Service, a trusted, high-quality source: the next 24 hours free; the full 6-day forecast with GoSki PRO. The snow report shows new snow, base depth, the season total and surface conditions.

How much snow has Telluride Ski Resort had this season?

Open Telluride Ski Resort in GoSki for its season total, base depth and new snow, and see where it ranks among U.S. resorts on the Snow leaderboard, this season and over the past 24, 48 and 72 hours. Source: NOAA.

Is there a 3D trail map of Telluride Ski Resort?

Yes. GoSki has a 2D and 3D trail map of Telluride Ski Resort with 135 named trails and 15 lifts, glades and search. The 2D map is free; 3D comes with GoSki PRO, and you can try three 3D maps free.

Can I track my ski runs at Telluride Ski Resort?

Yes. GoSki’s ski tracker names every run at Telluride Ski Resort by the trails you skied, with your vertical, speed and distance (GoSki PRO, on iPhone).

Can I get snow alerts for Telluride Ski Resort?

Yes. The free Snow Alert tells you when snow first shows up in the Telluride Ski Resort 7-day forecast, and Storm Alerts follow each storm from the first outlook to the final update (later updates are GoSki PRO).

Which ski passes include Telluride Ski Resort?

Telluride Ski Resort is on the Epic pass for the 2026-27 season, as listed in GoSki. Add your pass in GoSki and the resorts it covers are marked across the app.

Does Telluride Ski Resort have night skiing?

GoSki doesn’t list night skiing at Telluride Ski Resort for the 2026-27 season.

Where can I eat and après at Telluride Ski Resort?

GoSki lists top food and après picks on the mountain and nearby at Telluride Ski Resort.
