Jay Peak in GoSki

  • Snow conditions: new snow, base depth, season total and surface conditions.
  • Forecast: hourly snow, temperature and wind from the National Weather Service, a trusted, high-quality source; the next 24 hours free; the full 6-day forecast with GoSki PRO.
  • Snow leaderboard: where Jay Peak ranks among U.S. resorts for NOAA’s new snow at mid-mountain, this season and over the past 72, 48 and 24 hours.
  • Trail map: Jay Peak in 2D and 3D with 84 named trails and 8 lifts, glades and tree areas, and search.
  • Ski Tracker: record a day at Jay Peak and each run is named by the Jay Peak trails it followed. The day is named Jay Peak automatically from your GPS (GoSki PRO).
  • Storm Alerts and Snow Alert for Jay Peak, from the first sign of snow in the 7-day forecast.
  • Your pass: add your Indy pass in GoSki and the resorts it covers are marked across the app.
  • Food & Après Ski: top picks on the mountain and nearby.
