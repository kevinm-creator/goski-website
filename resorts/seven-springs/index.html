Seven Springs questions

Where can I check the Seven Springs snow forecast?

In GoSki, free on the App Store and Google Play. The Seven Springs forecast gives snow, temperature and wind from the National Weather Service, a trusted, high-quality source: the full 7-day forecast, free. The snow report shows new snow, base depth, the season total and surface conditions.

How much snow has Seven Springs had this season?

Open Seven Springs in GoSki for its season total, base depth and new snow, and see where it ranks among U.S. resorts on the Snow leaderboard, this season and over the past 24, 48 and 72 hours. Source: NOAA.

Is there a 3D trail map of Seven Springs?

Yes. GoSki has a 2D and 3D trail map of Seven Springs with 47 named trails and 14 lifts, glades and search. The 2D map is free; 3D comes with GoSki PRO, and you can try three 3D maps free.

Can I track my ski runs at Seven Springs?

Yes. GoSki’s ski tracker names every run at Seven Springs by the trails you skied, with your vertical, speed and distance (GoSki PRO, on iPhone).

Can I get snow alerts for Seven Springs?

Yes. The free Snow Alert tells you when snow first shows up in the Seven Springs 7-day forecast, and Storm Alerts follow each storm from the first outlook to the final update (later updates are GoSki PRO).

Which ski passes include Seven Springs?

Seven Springs is on the Epic pass for the 2026-27 season, as listed in GoSki. Add your pass in GoSki and the resorts it covers are marked across the app.

Does Seven Springs have night skiing?

Yes. GoSki lists night skiing at Seven Springs for the 2026-27 season; check the resort’s schedule before you go.

Where can I eat and après at Seven Springs?

GoSki lists top food and après picks on the mountain and nearby at Seven Springs.
