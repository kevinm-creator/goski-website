Sun Peaks Resort in GoSki

  • Snow conditions: new snow, base depth, season total and surface conditions.
  • Forecast: hourly snow, temperature and wind from NOAA’s National Blend of Models, with Environment Canada weather alerts, both trusted, high-quality sources; the next 24 hours free; the full 6-day forecast with GoSki PRO.
  • Trail map: Sun Peaks Resort in 2D and 3D with 150 named trails and 12 lifts, glades and tree areas, and search.
  • Ski Tracker: record a day at Sun Peaks Resort and each run is named by the Sun Peaks Resort trails it followed. The day is named Sun Peaks Resort automatically from your GPS (GoSki PRO).
  • Storm Alerts and Snow Alert for Sun Peaks Resort, from the first sign of snow in the 7-day forecast.
  • Your pass: add your Ikon or Mountain Collective pass in GoSki and the resorts it covers are marked across the app.
