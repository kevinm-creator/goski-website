Conditions on the 3D map

In GoSki 2.3 on the App Store and Google Play.

Conditions
Wind, powder, sun, snow depth and how soft the snow is, on the 3D trail map, hour by hour through the week (GoSki PRO).
Wind
How windy it is on every slope, and where it’s calm.
Powder Zone
Where the wind piled up fresh snow after a storm.
Sun
Which runs are in the sun and which are in the shade, hour by hour.
Snow depth
How much snow is on the ground, how much just fell and how much is coming.
Surface Conditions
Where the snow is firm, soft or heavy through the day, and where to ski when.

GoSki 2.2.1 to 2.2.8

On the App Store in October and November 2026.

GoSki PRO

One-tap ski tracking

Satellite Overlay

Smoother 3D maps

Clearer glades

Alerts

Resort list

GoSki 2.2

On the App Store since October 19, 2026, with the updates from October 5.

Trail maps for every resort
Trail maps went from 6 resorts to all 67, from Vail, Aspen Snowmass and Park City to Whistler Blackcomb, Revelstoke and Lake Louise.
Glades and tree skiing
Glades and tree areas marked on the maps.
Free 2D map, 3 free 3D maps
A clean 2D Basic map of every resort for everyone, and three free 3D maps.
Snow leaderboard
Every U.S. resort ranked by new snow. Source: NOAA.
Better ski tracking
Steadier barometer-backed vertical, current altitude, lift rides told apart from runs and trails skied at each resort.
Storm Alerts
Follow each storm at the resorts you pick, from the first outlook to the final update.
Day Alerts

App notifications and Snow Alert
Alerts by app notification or email, and a free Snow Alert.
