What’s new in GoSki

New features and improvements, newest first.

Updated October 24, 2026

Coming in the next update

In testing now.

GoSki PRO
GoSki’s subscription is now called GoSki PRO on every screen.
New Session start screen
A big round Start button, This Season and your Last Day. No resort to pick: each day is named from your GPS.
4K Terrain Overlay
A sharp aerial photo over the 3D map at 36 resorts, with the one-time 4K Map Pack. Download one mountain or all.
Smoother 3D maps
No more flat slabs when you zoom out or tilt, and soft glade edges on every map.
Glades redrawn
Glade and tree areas redrawn across the maps, with labels that never hide trail names.
Resort card
The Latest Resort Update at the top, with Day Alerts beside it.
Alerts
Day Alerts examples, two of each kind. Snow Alert and each storm’s first alert stay free; Day Alerts and a storm’s later updates are GoSki PRO.
You tab
The Activity log shows your three newest days, then View More.
Resort list
Sticky region tabs for Favorites and each region.
Settings
A Sign Out button above Delete Account.

GoSki 2.2

On the App Store since October 19, 2026, with the updates from October 5.

Trail maps for every resort
Trail maps went from 6 resorts to all 43, from Vail, Aspen Snowmass and Park City to Whistler Blackcomb, Revelstoke and Lake Louise.
Wooded areas and glades
Wooded areas filled with small trees, and glades shaded on the maps.
Free 2D map, 3 free 3D maps
A clean 2D Basic map of every resort for everyone, and three free 3D maps.
Snow leaderboard
Every U.S. resort ranked by NOAA’s new snow at mid-mountain.
Better ski tracking
Steadier barometer-backed vertical, current altitude, lift rides told apart from runs, trails skied per resort and new day cards.
Storm Alerts
One switch per resort, with storms told in parts, and a Storm Alerts card under the forecast.
Day Alerts
Lift alerts backed by Real Time data, Resort Updates through the day and a 3 PM Après message.
App notifications and Snow Alert
Alerts by app notification or email, and a free Snow Alert.
Sign in with Apple
On iPhone, alongside the 6-digit email code.
Your ski pass
Add your Epic, Ikon, Indy or Mountain Collective pass to see blackout dates, deals and opening days.
Ski regions and news
Pick your regions on a 3D map; resort news and updates for them on the Explore card.
Lift tickets and night skiing
Lift ticket prices with a link to buy, and night skiing noted for every resort.
Two new resorts
Crotched Mountain, New Hampshire, and Liberty Mountain, Pennsylvania.
Everyday polish
Food & Après Top Picks, this winter’s Season Total on resort cards, a frosted Filter & Sort sheet, MPH or km/h app-wide and swipe right to go back.
