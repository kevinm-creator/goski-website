Ski Tracker

A ski tracker that knows the trails

Tap Start and put your phone away. GoSki records your ski or snowboard day on iPhone and names every run by the trails it followed, at all 43 resorts with a GoSki trail map.

Updated October 24, 2026

Start, then forget about it

The Session tab has one big round Start button. You don’t pick a resort: GoSki knows which mountain you’re on and names the day for you, even without signal.

Lock your screen or switch apps and recording carries on; just don’t close GoSki. Your GoSki PRO membership is checked whenever you’re online, so a day can start in a lodge with no bars.

  • Keeps recording with the screen locked
  • Starts without signal
  • Encrypted on your iPhone, never uploaded

What it measures

Vertical skied
Downhill vertical on your runs only. Lift rides never add to it.
Runs and lift rides
Your day split into runs and lift rides, with Latest Runs while you ski.
Speed
Top speed and average moving speed, in MPH or km/h.
Distance and time
Distance skied, distance traveled with lift rides, total time and your time on snow across every season.
Current altitude
Your height above sea level while recording on iPhone.
Elevation and speed graphs
Scrub through the whole day and see where you were fastest.

Every run named, trail by trail

Each run lists the trails it followed in order, like Chute → Great Northern → Flume, with each trail’s rating and length, plus the run’s vertical, distance, time and top speed.

Each saved day gets a card with a small map of your runs, the date, vertical, runs, miles skied, top speed and total time.

App screen · sample winter conditions

Built for real mountains

Your day, drawn on the mountain

The route map shows your day on GoSki’s 3D trail map of the resort, colored by speed from green to red, with lift rides in gray. Tap Map All Runs to see the whole day, or one run to center it.

Steady vertical

GPS height drifts under trees and beside buildings. GoSki uses your iPhone’s barometer to keep vertical steady, so your numbers match the skiing you actually did.

Lift rides aren’t runs

GoSki recognizes lift rides, including flat lifts, lift stops and gondolas ridden back down, so none of them turns into a fake run or extra vertical.

Your season, adding up

The You tab totals your season: ski days, vertical, runs and time on snow, with an activity log of every day. Trails skied counts the different trails you’ve skied at each resort, and opens that resort’s map with them highlighted.

Forgot to record? Add a past day and tap the trails you skied on the resort’s map, or draw each run top to bottom.

App screen · sample winter conditions

Your ski days stay yours

Recordings are encrypted on your iPhone and tied to the account that recorded them. GoSki doesn’t upload them and doesn’t keep a cloud copy. Share a day when you want to; delete it any time.

Part of GoSki PRO

The Ski Tracker is included with GoSki PRO, a weekly or monthly subscription (prices are shown in the app). Lots of GoSki works free, like 2D trail maps, Live Lift Status and snow reports.
