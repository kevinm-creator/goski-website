Alerts

Alerts that know the mountain

Lift on hold? Storm on the way? GoSki tells you about the resorts you follow, by app notification or email.

Updated October 24, 2026

Day Alerts

Pick the resort you’re skiing today or tomorrow and GoSki alerts you when a lift goes on hold, is delayed or closes early, backed by Real Time data, plus Resort Updates through the day and a 3 PM Après message when there’s something on. Day Alerts run through the ski day and stop at 5:30 PM.

Turn them on with the Day Alerts switch beside the Latest Resort Update. Day Alerts are part of GoSki PRO.

App screen · sample winter conditions

Storm Alerts

One switch per resort. When a storm shows up, GoSki tells it in parts:

  • New Storm Outlook: a new storm first appears in the 7-day forecast. Free.
  • Snowfall Update: the latest expected totals as the storm gets closer.
  • Watches & Warnings: official winter storm watches and warnings from the National Weather Service (Environment Canada in Canada).
  • Final Update: the last word, about a day before the snow starts.

Storm Alerts are built on forecasts from NOAA and the National Weather Service, trusted, high-quality sources for winter weather. The resort page shows a Storm Alerts card under the forecast, with examples and your last five alerts. Every part after the first is GoSki PRO.

Snow Alert

A free heads-up when snow first appears in a resort’s 7-day forecast, for as many resorts as you like.

Resort Updates

The Explore card brings Resort Updates for the regions you pick: new lifts, snowmaking upgrades, new terrain, opening days, events and deals, with storm updates as bands across the top.

How alerts arrive

Choose app notifications or email, and set Day Alerts apart from weather alerts in You → Alerts. Text-message alerts are on the way for GoSki PRO.
