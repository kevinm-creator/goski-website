Plan it

Know where it snowed, and where it’s about to.

Snow in the last 48 hours, snow in the next 48, lifts and trails open: GoSki lines up the numbers that matter for every resort, with your favorites on top. Switch the map to NOAA’s snowfall analysis to see where the storm actually landed.

  • Morning reports

    Each resort’s own report: new snow, base depth, season total and surface.

  • Hour by hour

    Snow, temperature and wind from National Weather Service forecasts, out to seven days with GoSki Pass.

  • Past seasons

    See how this winter stacks up against previous seasons’ snowfall.

Ski it

A trail map that knows what’s running.

Detailed trail maps in 2D or 3D, drawn from open map data. Where the resort reports it, every lift gets a light: running, on hold or closed.

  • Lift lights

    Green, amber or red at a glance.

  • Find anything

    Trails, lifts, lodges, restrooms and food.

  • Map key switches

    Show learning areas, night skiing and race courses.

Ski day recording

Make every run count.

Tap Start Ski Day and put your phone away. GoSki turns your GPS route into estimated runs, vertical, distance and time, and at resorts with GoSki trail maps it names the trails each run followed.

  • Keeps recording with the screen locked
  • Encrypted on your iPhone, never uploaded
  • Included with GoSki Pass

Relive it

Every run, every day, the whole season.

Replay any day on 3D terrain with your route colored by speed, then check the elevation and speed charts. Your season stacks up as you go: ski days, vertical and runs, saved on your phone.

More mountain. Less guesswork.

  • Beyond the boundary

    Search U.S. places, plan a backcountry route and download map areas for when there’s no signal.

  • After last chair

    Après-ski spots on the mountain and nearby, plus events, at supported resorts.

  • 30-day trends

    Snowfall, base depth and open lifts and trails over the past month.
