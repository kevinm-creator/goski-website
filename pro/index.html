How GoSki PRO works

  • Choose weekly or monthly in the app. Apple handles payment through your App Store account.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in your App Store account settings.
  • You need a GoSki account to subscribe; sign in with a 6-digit email code or with Apple.
  • Once checked online, GoSki PRO keeps working without signal until its renewal date.

The 4K Map Pack

A one-time purchase, separate from GoSki PRO. It adds the 4K Terrain Overlay, a sharp aerial photo over the 3D trail map, at 36 resorts, and lets you save those maps on your phone.
