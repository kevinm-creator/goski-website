GoSki PRO

Free to use. PRO when you want more.

How GoSki PRO works

  • Choose weekly or monthly in the app. Payment goes through your App Store or Google Play account.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • You need a GoSki account to subscribe; sign in with a 6-digit email code, or with Apple on iPhone.
  • GoSki PRO keeps working on the mountain without signal.

Satellite Overlay

Part of GoSki PRO: the real aerial photo over the 3D trail map at 59 resorts, online and saved on your phone for days with no signal. It used to be the separate 4K Map Pack; people who bought the pack keep it.
