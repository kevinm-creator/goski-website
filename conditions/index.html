Conditions · Coming soon

Read the mountain before you ride it

Coming in the next GoSki update: wind, powder, sun, snow depth and how soft the snow is, drawn right on the 3D trail map, hour by hour. One button, five overlays, part of GoSki PRO.

Updated November 16, 2026

One button, five overlays

In the next update, a Conditions button sits in the 3D trail map’s key row, beside Minimize Names. Tap it for a card of five picture tiles and tick the one you want: one overlay shows at a time, drawn over the trails, lifts and names.

Each overlay has its own small card with a key, and an hour bar that runs from today through the week, so you can check this afternoon or plan Saturday. Snow depth’s Past view shows what has already fallen, so it needs no hour bar.

  • Wind: wind speed on every slope, the windiest spots and where it’s calm
  • Powder Zone: where the wind piles up new snow, and where it strips it away
  • Sun: sun and shade on every run, hour by hour
  • Snow depth: snow on the ground and new snow, from the last 24 hours to 7 days, plus the forecast
  • Surface Conditions: icy to soft snow through the day, and where to ski when

Wind Coming soon

Wind speed on every slope, the windiest spots and where it’s calm.

Comet-shaped strokes show which way the wind blows and how hard, colored from under 10 mph to 40 mph and up, and a glow runs from out of the wind to most windy. The card gives the wind’s direction, speed and gusts. Slide through the week hour by hour and find a sheltered run for a cold, windy day.

Powder Zone Coming soon

Where the wind piles up new snow, and where it strips it away.

After a storm the wind moves snow around. Powder Zone shades the slopes where it has likely piled up (more powder, some powder) and where it has likely blown away (some bare, more bare), with the new snow and the wind beside it. Off-piste, wind-loaded slopes are avalanche terrain, so the card links to the avalanche forecast.

App screen · sample winter conditions

Sun Coming soon

Sun and shade on every run, hour by hour.

See which runs are in the sun and which are in shade, and where the sun is in the sky. Switch between the last 4 hours, now, the next 4 hours and an average day: the sunny side for a cold morning, or the shade when the snow turns heavy.

App screen · sample winter conditions

Snow depth Coming soon

Snow on the ground and new snow, from the last 24 hours to 7 days, plus the forecast.

Past shows snow that has already fallen: what’s on the ground, or the new snow from the last 24 hours, 48 hours or 7 days, across the whole mountain. Forecast is its own switch, with the snow still to come hour by hour. Snow depth comes from NOAA in the U.S. and Environment Canada in Canada, trusted, high-quality sources.

App screen · sample winter conditions

Surface Conditions Coming soon

Icy to soft snow through the day, and where to ski when.

As the sun works on the slopes, snow goes from icy and harder to softening, soft and then heavy. Surface Conditions colors every slope for the hour you pick and lists the runs that are soft now, and Where to ski suggests runs for the morning, midday and afternoon.

Part of GoSki PRO

The Conditions overlays are included with GoSki PRO, a weekly or monthly subscription (prices are shown in the app). On a free map the tiles still show what each overlay does, with a link to GoSki PRO.

Questions about Conditions

What are the Conditions overlays in GoSki?

Coming in the next update. One Conditions button on the 3D trail map opens five overlays: Wind, Powder Zone, Sun, Snow depth and Surface Conditions. Tick one and it’s drawn over the mountain, with its own key and an hour bar that runs through the week. They’re part of GoSki PRO. More about Conditions.

Can GoSki show the wind on a ski trail map?

Coming soon. The Wind overlay draws the wind’s direction and speed on every slope of the 3D trail map, with a glow from out of the wind to most windy, hour by hour, so you can find a sheltered run on a windy day (GoSki PRO).

Does GoSki show where the snow is soft or icy?

Coming soon. Surface Conditions colors every slope from icy and harder to softening, soft and heavy for the hour you pick, lists the runs that are soft now and suggests where to ski in the morning, midday and afternoon (GoSki PRO).

Can I see snow depth on the trail map?

Coming soon. Snow depth shows the snow on the ground, or the new snow from the last 24 hours, 48 hours or 7 days, across the 3D trail map, with a Forecast switch for the snow to come (GoSki PRO).
