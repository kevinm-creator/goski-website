Five ways to read the mountain

Before you click in, see how the day will feel up there: where it’s windy, where the powder is, which runs are in the sun, how deep the snow is and when it softens. Pick one and see it on GoSki’s 3D trail map of the resort, hour by hour from today through the week, so you can check this afternoon or plan Saturday.

  • Wind: how windy it is on every slope, and where it’s calm
  • Powder Zone: where the wind piled up fresh snow after a storm
  • Sun: which runs are in the sun and which are in the shade, hour by hour
  • Snow depth: how much snow is on the ground, how much just fell and how much is coming
  • Surface Conditions: where the snow is firm, soft or heavy through the day, and where to ski when

Wind

How windy it is on every slope, and where it’s calm.

Wind can turn a good day cold fast. See how hard it’s blowing across the mountain, from which direction, with gusts, and which runs are sheltered. Look ahead hour by hour through the week to plan around a windy morning or find a calm run when it picks up.

App screen · sample winter conditions

Powder Zone

Where the wind piled up fresh snow after a storm.

After a storm, the wind moves new snow around. Powder Zone shows where it likely piled up into deeper powder and where it blew slopes down to a firmer base, so you know where to look first. Wind-loaded slopes outside the ski area can be avalanche terrain, so GoSki links to the avalanche forecast: check it before you head out of bounds.

App screen · sample winter conditions

Sun

Which runs are in the sun and which are in the shade, hour by hour.

Chasing the sun on a cold morning, or shade when the snow turns heavy? See which runs get the sun and which sit in the shade at any hour of the day, and follow the light around the mountain.

App screen · sample winter conditions

Snow depth

How much snow is on the ground, how much just fell and how much is coming.

See how deep the snow is across the whole mountain, how much fell in the last day, two days or week, and how much more is on the way. Snow depth comes from NOAA in the U.S., Environment Canada in Canada and Europe’s national weather services in the Alps, trusted, high-quality sources.

App screen · sample winter conditions

Surface Conditions

Where the snow is firm, soft or heavy through the day, and where to ski when.

Snow changes through the day: firm or icy in the morning, softening as the sun warms it, heavy by late afternoon. See how the snow is likely to feel on each slope at the hour you pick, which runs are soft right now, and where to ski in the morning, midday and afternoon.

Questions about Conditions

What are Conditions in GoSki?

Five ways to read the mountain on the 3D trail map: Wind, Powder Zone, Sun, Snow depth and Surface Conditions, hour by hour through the week. They’re part of GoSki PRO. More about Conditions.

Can GoSki show the wind on a ski trail map?

Yes. GoSki shows how windy it is on every slope of the 3D trail map, the wind’s direction and gusts, and which runs are sheltered, hour by hour, so you can find a calm run on a windy day (GoSki PRO).

Where is the best powder after a storm?

Wind decides a lot of it. GoSki’s Powder Zone shows where the wind likely piled up new snow and where it blew it away, right on the 3D trail map (GoSki PRO).

Which ski runs get the most sun?

GoSki’s Sun view shows which runs are in the sun and which are in the shade at any hour, so you can chase the sun on a cold morning or find shade when the snow gets heavy (GoSki PRO).

Does GoSki show where the snow is soft or icy?

Yes. Surface Conditions shows where the snow is likely firm, softening, soft or heavy at the hour you pick, which runs are soft now and where to ski through the day (GoSki PRO).

Can I see snow depth on the trail map?

Yes. See how much snow is on the ground across the 3D trail map, how much fell in the last 24 hours, 48 hours or 7 days, and how much is in the forecast (GoSki PRO).
