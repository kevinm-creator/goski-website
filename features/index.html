Features

Everything in GoSki

GoSki is a free app for skiers and snowboarders on iPhone, iPad and Android. It covers 87 ski resorts: 54 in the U.S., 13 in Canada and 20 in the Alps, with a trail map of every one.

Updated November 23, 2026

Snow forecasts and reports

Snow reports
New snow, base depth, season total and surface conditions, with lifts and trails open, for every resort in the U.S. and Canada and most in the Alps.
Hourly forecast
Snow, temperature and wind for every resort: the full 7-day forecast, free. From NOAA and the National Weather Service, with Environment Canada weather alerts in Canada, and from Europe’s national weather services in the Alps, trusted, high-quality sources.
Weather warnings
Winter storm watches and warnings in the U.S. and Canada, and snow and ice warnings in the Alps, right on the resort page.
Snowfall maps
See where it snowed over the last 24 hours to 7 days across the U.S., from NOAA, and snow forecast maps for the next 72 hours, the Alps included.
Snow leaderboard
Which U.S. resorts got the most snow: this season and the past 24, 48 and 72 hours. Source: NOAA.
Season totals
This winter’s season total on resort cards, and past seasons’ snowfall at U.S. resorts.
30-day trends
Snow base over the past month, free; open lifts and trails too with GoSki PRO.

Ski Tracker

The Ski Tracker is part of GoSki PRO and records on iPhone. How the Ski Tracker works

One tap to start
Tap Start and put your phone away. GoSki knows which resort you’re at, so there’s nothing to pick.
Runs named by trail
Every run is named by the trails you skied, at all 87 resorts, and shown on the resort’s trail map.
Steady vertical
Uses your iPhone’s barometer, so vertical stays steady even under trees and near buildings.
Lift rides aren’t runs
Lift rides, gondolas included, never count as runs or vertical, so your numbers are your skiing.
Relive the day
Your route colored by speed, elevation and speed graphs, top speed and time on snow.
Your season
Ski days, vertical, runs and trails skied at each resort add up all season. Forgot to record? Add the day afterwards.

Alerts

More about alerts

Day Alerts
Follow the resort you’re skiing today or tomorrow: lift holds, delays and closures backed by Real Time data, Resort Updates through the day and a 3 PM Après message (GoSki PRO).
Storm Alerts
A storm told in parts for the resorts you follow: New Storm Outlook, Snowfall Update, Watches & Warnings and a Final Update. Each storm’s first alert is free.
Snow Alert
A free heads-up when snow first appears in a resort’s 7-day forecast.
Your choice of delivery
App notifications or email. Text messages are on the way for GoSki PRO.

Plan your day

Your ski pass
Add your Epic, Ikon, Indy or Mountain Collective pass. GoSki marks the resorts it covers and shows blackout dates on a calendar, plus deals and opening days.
Resort updates and ski news
New lifts, snowmaking, new terrain, opening days, events and deals for the regions you pick, plus storm updates.
Ski regions
Pick the regions you ski and their resorts come first, with your favorites close at hand.
North America or the Alps
Switch continents and GoSki shows that side’s resorts and ski regions. Search still finds every resort.
Lift tickets and night skiing
Lift ticket prices with a quick link to buy, and night skiing noted for every resort.
Food & Après Ski
Top picks on the mountain and nearby at every resort, with travel estimates.
Backcountry
Avalanche danger in the U.S., Canada and the Alps, U.S. place search that works offline, offline map areas you choose, and routes with distance, an elevation profile and an estimated hike time.

Account and privacy

Read the privacy policy

No password
Sign in with a 6-digit code sent to your email, or with Sign in with Apple on iPhone.
Recordings stay on your phone
Ski day recordings are encrypted on your iPhone and are never uploaded.
No ad tracking
GoSki doesn’t sell your data or track you across other apps for ads.
Imperial or metric
Inches, feet, mph and °F, or centimeters, meters, km/h and °C. Auto uses metric in the Alps and imperial in North America, or pick one for the whole app.
