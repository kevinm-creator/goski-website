Trail Maps

3D trail maps for all 43 resorts

Every resort in GoSki has its own trail map, in 2D and in 3D on real terrain: 31 in the U.S. and 12 in Canada. Built from open map data, with names you can read up close.

Updated October 24, 2026

What’s on the map

Live Lift Status
Backed by Real Time data: green running, amber on hold, red closed.
Trails by rating
Green circle, blue square and black diamond trails, each with its name.
Glades and tree areas
Wooded areas filled with small trees, and glades shaded with their names.
Closed and boundary areas
Faint stripes for closed areas and clear ski area boundaries, gates and hiking routes.
Search
Trails, lifts, lodges, food and parking.
Map key switches
Learning areas, night skiing, race courses and magic carpets.
Tap a run or lift
Its name and length, traced in yellow.
2D or 3D
Tilt and turn the mountain in 3D, or flip to a flat 2D map.

App screen · sample winter conditions

4K Terrain Overlay

The 4K Map Pack drapes a sharp aerial photo over the 3D map at 36 resorts, so you can see the real tree lines, cliffs and lift lines. It’s a one-time purchase, separate from GoSki PRO. Download one mountain or all of them, over Wi-Fi or cellular, and they’re saved on your phone.

Not in the pack: Revelstoke Mountain Resort, Kicking Horse Mountain Resort, Fernie Alpine Resort, Sun Peaks Resort, Big White Ski Resort, RED Mountain Resort, Panorama Mountain Resort.

Free and GoSki PRO

Without GoSki PRO you get a clean 2D Basic trail map of every resort, with trail colors and names, lifts, Live Lift Status and boundaries, plus three free 3D maps, each one resort for 24 hours. GoSki PRO opens the full 3D map everywhere.
