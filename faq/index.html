Questions, answered

The short answers about GoSki. Still stuck? Contact support.

Updated November 23, 2026

What is GoSki?

GoSki is a free app for skiers and snowboarders on iPhone, iPad and Android, from the App Store and Google Play. It has snow forecasts for 87 ski resorts: 54 in the U.S., 13 in Canada and 20 in the Alps, a 2D and 3D trail map of every one, snow reports, Live Lift Status backed by Real Time data, storm and lift alerts, and a GPS ski tracker that names every run by trail.

Is GoSki free?

Yes. GoSki is free to download and use: snow reports, the 7-day forecast, Ski Tracker Basic, 2D trail maps of every resort plus three free 3D maps, Live Lift Status, snowfall maps, the Snow leaderboard, ski news, the Snow Alert and each storm’s first alert are all free. GoSki PRO is an optional weekly or monthly subscription.

What does GoSki PRO include?

Ski Tracker PLUS (runs on the trail map, speed heat map, live playback, graphs and the trail-by-trail breakdown), 3D trail maps at every resort, Satellite Overlay, Mountain Intel (Wind, Powder Zone, Sun, Snow depth and Surface Conditions), Live Radar, Day Alerts, every Storm Alert, text message alerts and full 30-day trends. Prices are shown in the app.

Which ski resorts does GoSki cover?

87 resorts: 54 in the U.S. (Colorado, Vermont, Utah, California, New Hampshire, New York, Pennsylvania, Maine, Wyoming, Washington, Nevada, Montana, Idaho and West Virginia), 13 in Canada (British Columbia, Alberta and Quebec) and 20 in the Alps (France, Austria, Italy and Switzerland). See the full list on the resorts page.

Does GoSki have 3D trail maps?

Yes, for all 87 resorts. Without GoSki PRO you get a 2D Basic map of every resort and three free 3D maps, each for 24 hours; GoSki PRO opens 3D everywhere, with Satellite Overlay, the real aerial photo, at 76 of them.

What are Conditions in GoSki?

Five ways to read the mountain on the 3D trail map: Wind, Powder Zone, Sun, Snow depth and Surface Conditions, hour by hour through the week. They’re part of GoSki PRO. More about Conditions.

Can GoSki show the wind on a ski trail map?

Yes. GoSki shows how windy it is on every slope of the 3D trail map, the wind’s direction and gusts, and which runs are sheltered, hour by hour, so you can find a calm run on a windy day (GoSki PRO).

Where is the best powder after a storm?

Wind decides a lot of it. GoSki’s Powder Zone shows where the wind likely piled up new snow and where it blew it away, right on the 3D trail map (GoSki PRO).

Which ski runs get the most sun?

GoSki’s Sun view shows which runs are in the sun and which are in the shade at any hour, so you can chase the sun on a cold morning or find shade when the snow gets heavy (GoSki PRO).

Does GoSki show where the snow is soft or icy?

Yes. Surface Conditions shows where the snow is likely firm, softening, soft or heavy at the hour you pick, which runs are soft now and where to ski through the day (GoSki PRO).

Can I see snow depth on the trail map?

Yes. See how much snow is on the ground across the 3D trail map, how much fell in the last 24 hours, 48 hours or 7 days, and how much is in the forecast (GoSki PRO).

How does the GoSki ski tracker work?

Tap Start and put your phone away. GoSki records your day on your iPhone, splits it into runs and lift rides, and names each run by the trails you skied. It shows vertical, runs, speed, distance and time, live and afterwards.

Does the ski tracker work without cell signal?

Yes. Recording and run naming work without cell signal, and you can start a day with no bars.

Does GoSki count lift rides in my vertical?

No. Vertical skied counts the downhill on your runs only; lift rides, gondolas included, never count.

How does GoSki keep vertical accurate?

GoSki uses your iPhone’s barometer, which follows height changes more steadily than GPS alone, especially under trees and near buildings, and it counts only the downhill on your runs.

Can I use GoSki for snowboarding?

Yes. The tracker, maps, forecasts and alerts work the same for skiers and snowboarders.

Is my location data private?

Ski day recordings are encrypted on your iPhone and are never uploaded; there’s no cloud copy. GoSki doesn’t sell your data or track you across other apps for ads. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does GoSki show live lift status?

Yes. Live Lift Status, backed by Real Time data, shows each lift on the trail map as running (green), on hold (amber) or closed (red). With GoSki PRO, Day Alerts tell you when a lift at the resort you’re skiing goes on hold, is delayed or closes early.

Where does GoSki’s weather come from?

From trusted, high-quality sources: NOAA and the National Weather Service in the U.S., NOAA with Environment Canada weather alerts in Canada, and Europe’s national weather services in the Alps. Past snowfall maps and the Snow leaderboard use NOAA snowfall data.

Can GoSki alert me when it’s going to snow?

Yes. The free Snow Alert tells you when snow first appears in a resort’s 7-day forecast, and Storm Alerts follow a storm in parts: New Storm Outlook (free), Snowfall Update, Watches & Warnings and a Final Update (GoSki PRO).

Can I add my Epic, Ikon, Indy or Mountain Collective pass?

Yes. Add your pass and GoSki marks the resorts it covers, shows blackout dates on a calendar for each resort and lists deals and opening days for your pass.

Does GoSki work in Canada?

Yes. GoSki covers 13 resorts in British Columbia, Alberta and Quebec, with trusted forecasts from NOAA and Environment Canada weather alerts. Snowfall maps and the Snow leaderboard cover U.S. resorts only.

Does GoSki work in Europe?

Yes, in the Alps. GoSki covers 20 resorts: Chamonix-Mont-Blanc, Courchevel, Val Thorens, Méribel, La Plagne, Les Arcs, Alpe d'Huez and Tignes in France; St. Anton am Arlberg, Kitzbühel, Saalbach Skicircus, Ischgl / Samnaun and SkiWelt in Austria; Cortina d'Ampezzo, Val Gardena, Via Lattea (Sestriere) and Alta Badia in Italy; Zermatt, Verbier and St. Moritz in Switzerland. Each has a 2D and 3D trail map and the 7-day forecast, with snow and ice warnings and the avalanche danger, in metric units unless you pick imperial. Switch between North America and the Alps in the app. Past snowfall maps and the Snow leaderboard cover U.S. resorts only.

Does GoSki use metric units?

Yes. Pick Auto, Imperial or Metric in Settings. Auto shows centimeters, meters, km/h and °C when you’re looking at the Alps, and inches, feet, mph and °F in North America. You can also flip any 7-day forecast between °F and °C.

Is GoSki on Android?

Yes. GoSki is on Google Play for Android phones (Android 7.0 or later) and on the App Store for iPhone and iPad (iOS 15 or later).

Can I record a day I forgot to track?

Yes. Add the day afterwards by picking the trails you skied on the resort’s map. It counts toward your season.

How do I sign in?

There’s no password. Sign in with a 6-digit code sent to your email, or with Sign in with Apple on iPhone.

Is GoSki affiliated with any ski resort?

No. GoSki is independent and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by any resort.
