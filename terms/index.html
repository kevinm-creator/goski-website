  • Consent: you get texts only after you enter your number in the app and tick the box agreeing to them. Agreeing is not a condition of any purchase.
  • How often: message frequency varies with conditions at the resorts you follow, up to 6 messages a day. No texts are sent during your quiet hours.
  • Cost: GoSki doesn’t charge for texts, but message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.

Alert emails include an unsubscribe link, and you can turn email alerts off on the Alerts screen.
