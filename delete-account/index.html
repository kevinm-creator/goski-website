Account

Delete GoSki Account

App: GoSki · Developer: The GoSki Team

Updated October 24, 2026

In the App

  1. Open GoSki and sign in.
  2. Tap You at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Account & privacy.
  4. Under Out of bounds, tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm when asked.

Signed in with Apple? Apple asks you to confirm too. Export any ski days you want to keep first.

By Email

Can’t open the app? Email goskiapp@gmail.com from the address you sign in with and ask us to delete your GoSki account. We aim to reply within 24–48 hours.

What’s Deleted

  • Your sign-in and profile
  • Favorites
  • Snow alerts
  • The phone number for text alerts
  • Notification sign-ups
  • Resort requests
  • Ski days saved on the phone you delete from, and any day being recorded

What Stays

  • Store subscriptions. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel them. Cancel in your Apple, Google Play or Samsung Galaxy Store account.
  • Purchase records. Each store keeps its own. RevenueCat, which checks GoSki purchases, may keep them too, for checking and restoring purchases.
  • Text sign-up check. A scrambled copy of a text-alert phone number, for up to 30 days, to stop sign-up abuse.
  • Exported files. Ski days you exported stay where you saved them.
  • Ski days on a phone. They’re never uploaded. Deleting in the app erases them from that phone; an email request can’t reach them.
